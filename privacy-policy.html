Privacy Policy

Task Desk

Last Updated: December 12, 2025

1. Introduction

Task Desk ("we," "our," or "us"), developed by Jaka Kavčič, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application (the "App") handles your information. We believe in transparency and want you to understand that we do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to our servers. All your information stays on your device and in your personal iCloud account.

2. Information Storage

2.1 Local Storage

All tasks, settings, and app data you create are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you enable iCloud sync, in which case it is stored in your personal iCloud account that only you control.

2.2 iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your tasks and app data are synchronized across your devices (iPhone, iPad, and Mac) using Apple's iCloud service. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

2.3 Calendar Integration

Task Desk can integrate with your device's calendar using iCalendar capabilities. Calendar data accessed by the App remains on your device and is used solely to help you manage your tasks. We do not collect or transmit your calendar information.

3. Permissions We Request

3.1 Location (Optional)

If you grant permission, Task Desk may access your location to provide context-aware task suggestions and improve task accuracy. Location data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party. You can revoke location permission at any time in your device's Settings.

3.2 Notifications

Task Desk sends local notifications to remind you about your tasks. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device. We do not use push notification servers or collect any notification-related data.

3.3 Calendar Access

If you grant permission, Task Desk can read and write to your device's calendar to sync tasks with your calendar events. This data remains on your device and in your iCloud account.

4. AI Features

Task Desk uses AI-powered features to enhance your task management experience and improve efficiency. These AI features process data locally on your device to provide personalized suggestions and predictions. We do not send your task data to external AI services or servers.

5. Data We Do NOT Collect

To be clear, Task Desk does NOT collect:

• Your tasks or task content
• Your location data
• Your calendar information
• Your personal information
• Usage analytics or behavioral data
• Device identifiers for tracking purposes
• Any data that could identify you personally

6. Third-Party Services

6.1 Apple Services

Task Desk integrates with Apple services including iCloud for data sync, Apple Calendar for calendar integration, and the App Store for in-app purchases (tips). Your use of these services is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policies.

6.2 In-App Purchases

Task Desk offers optional tip functionality through Apple's In-App Purchase system. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive, store, or have access to your payment information.

7. Data Security

Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features and, if you use iCloud sync, by Apple's iCloud security measures. Since we do not collect or store your data on our servers, there is no risk of your data being compromised through our systems.

8. Children's Privacy

Task Desk does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The App can be used by people of all ages. Since all data remains on-device, there are no special concerns regarding children's data collection.

9. Your Rights

Since all your data is stored locally on your device and in your personal iCloud account, you have complete control over your data. You can:

• View all your data within the App
• Delete your data at any time by deleting tasks or uninstalling the App
• Disable iCloud sync to keep data only on your device
• Revoke any permissions (location, calendar, notifications) in device Settings
• Export your data using iOS sharing features

10. European Users (GDPR)

Task Desk is developed in Slovenia, a member of the European Union. Since we do not collect any personal data, GDPR compliance is straightforward: there is no personal data processing to regulate. You maintain full control of your data at all times.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the App and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
